## Public Large-Board Baseline Harness

Captured post-floor baselines after repinning to `kicad-monkey 2026.7.17`, using the same harness, boards, workload list, round count, and max copper layer setting as the pre-floor run.

Commands run:

Interpretation:

- The public Monkey release produces measurable improvements in Cruncher without local implementation: headline parse-like workflows improved by roughly 1.25x to 1.39x, and one cached SVG composition case improved by 1.76x.
- The `design/dr` PCB review SVG loop did not materially improve. That keeps a Cruncher-local command-scoped render cache candidate in scope for review.
- These are research baselines, not release gates. They are like-for-like within this harness; future release-note claims should cite the same harness and corpus.
